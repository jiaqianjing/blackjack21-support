# Blackjack 21 IQ

**Think the hand. Learn the odds.**

Blackjack 21 IQ is an offline iOS blackjack strategy and probability trainer. It combines complete virtual-chip play with basic-strategy guidance and shoe-aware probability insights, helping players understand each decision instead of simply memorizing a chart.

[Visit the official product page](https://jiaqianjing.github.io/blackjack21-support/)

## What it offers

- Complete blackjack hands with configurable table rules
- Basic-strategy recommendations for currently legal actions
- Dealer outcome, bust-risk, and expected-value estimates
- English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese interfaces
- Offline play with virtual chips only
- No account, advertising, analytics, or tracking
